How It Works

Simple, accountable 4-stage civic resolution flow

1

Spot & Capture

Take a photo of the road damage and record the street location.

2

Submit Ticket

Upload evidence to generate an auto-incremented complaint tracking ID.

3

Inspection

Municipal road authorities review damage severity and dispatch crews.

4

Resolution

Road repairs are completed and status transitions dynamically to Resolved.
